Our story

Engineering vessels that perform

We are a premier naval architecture and marine engineering consultancy delivering advanced, cross-disciplinary design solutions, pressure vessel engineering, and technical performance optimization for the global maritime industry.

Our work spans the full breadth of the maritime world — from yachts, boats and sailing craft to ships, container ships, barges and pontoons, and on to the next generation of unmanned surface and underwater vehicles. Whatever the platform, we bring the same rigor: sound engineering, careful optimization, and designs built to standard.
