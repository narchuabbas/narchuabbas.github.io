Who we are

Cross-disciplinary design, from concept to classification

We combine naval architecture, marine engineering and pressure-vessel expertise to design vessels and unmanned platforms that perform — efficiently, safely and to standard.

From yachts and commercial ships to USVs, UUVs and 3D-printable models, our team supports every stage of the design spiral. Based in Karachi, Pakistan, we serve clients across the global maritime industry.
